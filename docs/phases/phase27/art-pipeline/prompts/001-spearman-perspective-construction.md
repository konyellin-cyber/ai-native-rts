# 001 Spearman Perspective Construction

## Goal

Validate the Phase 26 spearman perspective before any finished art is produced.

This step must create a sketch-like construction drawing that proves the 45-degree RTS camera, human body proportion, foot placement, and spear direction are correct. It is not an animation sheet and not final art.

## Positive Prompt

Create a clean pencil/ink construction sketch for a single medieval spearman viewed from a 45-degree orthographic RTS camera.

Purpose:
- This is a perspective and anatomy construction sheet, not finished game art.
- Show the body as simple construction forms: head sphere, chest box, pelvis block, limb cylinders, joint marks, foot contact points.
- Show light construction guide lines for the ground plane: a small isometric floor grid or ellipse under the feet.
- Show one long spear as a simple straight construction line lying in the same ground-plane direction used by a Phase 26 front-line formation.
- The pose should be a neutral ready stance suitable for a dense spear wall.
- The viewer should see helmet/top-of-head direction, shoulders, torso top plane, and both feet placed on the ground.
- Keep proportions grounded and non-heroic, suitable for a mass soldier.

Camera and composition:
- 45-degree orthographic RTS view.
- No side-view, no horizontal brawler view, no front-facing character sheet.
- The body should face diagonally across the ground plane.
- White or very light gray paper background.
- Black, gray, and blue construction lines only.

Style:
- rough but readable animation production sketch
- line art only
- no color fill
- no material rendering
- no shadows
- no armor details beyond very simple helmet/chest placeholders
- no text labels
- no watermark

## Negative Prompt

Avoid finished colored art, fully rendered armor, dramatic lighting, shadows, painterly style, side-view sprites, front-facing pose, strong wide-angle perspective, fantasy heroic proportions, oversized armor, background scenery, UI, text labels, multiple characters, animation frames, cropped feet, cropped spear.
